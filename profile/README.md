# BHOS CE24
## The Holy CE

BHOS CE24 is a student-run Computer Engineering community at Baku Higher Oil School.

We are a team of computer engineering students at BHOS whose goal is to develop free and open software.

---

## The Ten Commandments of the Holy CE

I. Thou shalt leave the codebase better than thou found it.  
II. Thou shalt favor clarity over cleverness.  
III. Thou shalt seek understanding before correction.  
IV. Thou shalt document thy work, lest knowledge perish with thee.  
V. Thou shalt disagree with ideas, not with people.  
VI. Thou shalt value learning above pride.  
VII. Thou shalt give credit where it is due.  
VIII. Thou shalt treat every member with dignity and respect.  
IX. Thou shalt share thy knowledge freely.  
X. Thou shalt not forsake truth for convenience.
